## What actually happened

We decided to catch up at our previous workplace, Mamasita, which gave me an opportunity to present my creative coding pieces to them. Honestly, I felt nervous, especially since our book club hasn’t met up recently and I didn’t want them to feel uncomfortable that I was recording them. I was pleasantly surprised with their reactions, as they were engaging with each of the artworks and explaining the imagery associated for them personally. This was very rewarding to witness, although I think the act of recording it for a university project potentially heightened their genuine reactions.

## What was interesting about the experience

This project provided me with an opportunity to explore some of my favourite books in more depth and incorporate this passion into a digital artwork. Admittedly, the most rewarding aspect of the experience was watching my book club respond to these pieces, and it made me incredibly grateful for my friends and the time that we spend together. I loved watching back the footage that I recorded of us laughing and chatting, and I even discovered that one of the book club members, George, doesn’t overly enjoy reading, but agreed to join our book club as an excuse to hang out more frequently. I was surprised by my enjoyment producing the p5.js sketches, as I was able to utilise code to create pieces that were meaningful to me, and it made me realise how much of an inspiration that books could impart on all of my creative endeavours moving forward.
